![PA-18 Super Cub on the runway](/projects/rcwebsim/screenshot.jpg)

A browser RC flight simulator. You fly a 1.7 m foam PA-18 Super Cub from the
pilot station, just like at the field. A custom aerodynamics model makes it fly
like the real foam model.

Keyboard, gamepads and USB RC transmitters are supported.

## Stack

Vue · three.js (WebGPU) · [Rapier](https://rapier.rs/) · custom aerodynamics model
